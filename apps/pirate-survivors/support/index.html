Pirate Survivors: Cursed Seas

Support

Need help with Pirate Survivors: Cursed Seas? Send a message to @florianldt on X (Twitter).

To help track down an issue, please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, the game version, and what happened. Steps to reproduce it and a screenshot are a big help.

TestFlight testers can also send feedback directly from TestFlight.

How to play

Choose a ship with the < and > arrows on the title screen (all five are free), then tap Set Sail. Your ship casts off from the home port; tap through the Captain’s dialogue. Touch and drag anywhere in the game area to steer; your weapons fire automatically. Collect doubloons, and tap a blessing each time you level up.

Game controllers

Use the left stick to steer, the d-pad to move through menus, and the south face button (✕ on PlayStation, A on Xbox) to confirm. Menu highlights appear while a controller is connected.

Settings and scores

Use the pause button to resume, check your stats or return to port. Music, sound effects, menu sounds and haptics each have their own setting; haptics need a supported device. Your scores are kept on your device in the Captain’s Ledger.
