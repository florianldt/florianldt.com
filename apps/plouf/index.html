Free on iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.

Remember the water ring toy you could never put down? Plouf brings it to your phone. Press the pump, watch the pieces swirl and float, and land each one on the pole to rebuild a sticker character.

No ads, no account, no subscription. Play offline on iPhone.

Press the pump

Every press shoots a jet of bubbles through the tank. Press harder to push more water and toss the pieces higher.

Land them on the pole

Each character is cut into tiles. Splash them onto the pole from the feet up, the right way round, until the picture is whole.

Collect 120 stickers

Finish a level to win its sticker, faster for up to three stars. Ten worlds of twelve, from the Ocean and Space to Dino Land and the City.

A twist in every world

Low gravity, slippery sweets, earthquakes, a weaker pump, icy water and city currents keep every world feeling new.

Tilt, shake, pause

Tilt your phone to slosh the water toward a stuck piece, shake to start over, and pause any time with a tap.

Your toy, your way

Pick the hand the pump sits under and play in English or Korean. The first world is free; one Full Toy Box purchase opens the rest and five more colorways.
