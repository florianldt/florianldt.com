_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

This policy covers **Plouf: Water Toy** for iPhone, developed by Florian Ludot.

## In short

Plouf has no accounts, no ads and no tracking across apps. It collects anonymous usage data so the levels can be tuned, and you can turn that off in Settings at any time. It never collects your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

## Data stored on your device

Your progress (stars, best times and collected stickers), your current level and your settings (sound, tilt, pump hand and colorway) are saved on your device. They are not sent to the developer.

Your device may include this data in backups made by iCloud or your computer. Those backups are handled by Apple or by you, not by the developer. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Anonymous usage data

When **Share anonymous usage data** is on in Settings (the default), the app sends anonymous gameplay events to [PostHog](https://posthog.com), the analytics service the developer uses: for example, which level was started or finished, how long it took and how many stars it earned, which screens were opened, Settings changes, and whether the Full Toy Box offer was viewed or bought. If the app crashes, a crash report is sent the next time it opens.

These events are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to you. They include technical details such as the device model, iOS version, app version and language. They do not include your name, email, contacts, photos, location or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and they are never used for advertising or sold.

Turn **Share anonymous usage data** off in Settings and nothing more is collected or sent.

## Purchases

The optional **Full Toy Box** is bought through Apple's App Store; the developer never sees your payment details. The app uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com) to check whether you own it and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier and the App Store receipt, under [its own privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Children

Plouf is made to be enjoyed by all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: usage data is anonymous and can be turned off in Settings.

## Beta testing

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may collect diagnostics, crash reports and feedback under its own terms and share them with the developer, who uses them only to fix bugs and improve the game. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support and feedback

If you contact the developer by email or on X (Twitter), the developer receives your address or username and whatever you choose to include, and uses it only to reply and investigate the issue.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
