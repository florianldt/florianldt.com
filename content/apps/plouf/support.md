## How to play

Press the big pump button to shoot water into the tank; press harder for a stronger jet. Toss each piece so its slot drops onto the pole, stacking from the bottom up with every piece the right way round. The faded picture in the tank shows where each piece goes, and the bars under the tank fill as pieces lock in. Finish the stack to win the level's sticker; finish faster for more stars.

Tilt your phone to slosh the water toward a stuck piece. Shake it to knock every piece loose and start the figure over.

## Pausing

Tap the clock at the top of the screen to pause, and tap **Resume** or the pump to carry on. The game also pauses by itself when you open Settings or the Sticker Album, or leave the app.

## Sticker Album

Tap the book at the top left to see every world and sticker, replay any level you've collected, or continue from the next one. Levels open one at a time, and each world opens when you've collected every sticker in the world before it.

## Full Toy Box

The Ocean world is free. The Full Toy Box is a one-time purchase that opens every other world, every future world and five more colorways for the toy. To restore it on a new device, open **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases**.

## Settings

Choose which hand the pump sits under, turn sound and tilt on or off, pick a colorway and turn anonymous usage data on or off. The app follows your iPhone's language (English or Korean).
