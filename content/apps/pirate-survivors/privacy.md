_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

This policy covers **Pirate Survivors: Cursed Seas** for iPhone and iPad, developed by Florian Ludot.

## In short

The game does not collect any personal data. It works offline, needs no account, and contains no ads, tracking, third-party analytics or in-app purchases.

## Data stored on your device

Your scores, survival times, levels, kill counts, run dates, chosen ship, Endless unlock and sound and haptics settings are saved locally on your device so the game can show your Captain's Ledger and remember your preferences. This information is never sent to the developer or to any server operated for the game.

Your device may include this data in backups made by iCloud or your computer. Those backups are handled by Apple or by you, not by the developer.

You can delete all of this data at any time by deleting the app.

## Support and feedback

If you contact the developer on X (Twitter), the developer receives your username and whatever you choose to include, and uses it only to reply and investigate the issue. Your message is also handled by X under [its own privacy policy](https://x.com/privacy). Please don't include personal information that isn't needed.

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may collect diagnostics, crash reports and feedback under its own terms. Information that Apple shares with the developer is used only to fix bugs and improve the game. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
