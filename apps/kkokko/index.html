Kkokko: Rooftop Hen

A hen on a Seoul rooftop. Look in for a minute, then go about your day.

Coming soon to the App Store

A hen lives on a rooftop in Seoul. Fill her feeder, pick up the egg she lays, and leave it in the carton by the door for the neighbors.

Free on iPhone, with optional coin packs for the corner shop. No account and no ads.

Real time, real weather

Everything happens on the real clock. The sky is Seoul's real weather, hour by hour, through four seasons of cherry petals, ginkgo leaves and snow.

Neighbors who write back

Halmeoni Park, Minji, Mr. Oh and Jun come by at their own hours for an egg, and leave handwritten notes, in English or Korean, and sometimes a gift.

Keepsakes with a story

Twenty-four trinkets, each with a memento, for friendship and for Seollal, Chuseok and Christmas. Sixteen kinds of egg, depending on her mood and her treats.

A little roof of your own

Grow tomatoes, corn and sunflowers in the planters, hand her the harvest, and spend the coins from thank-you notes at the corner shop.

On your home screen

A widget keeps her with you, asleep at night, with a note beside her when something is waiting by the door.

Being away is fine

No streaks, no timers, no red dots. Miss a few days and she'll be all right; the eggs you missed wait in your tray.
