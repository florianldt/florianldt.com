Pirate Survivors: Cursed Seas

Sail into cursed waters. Build your arsenal. Survive the swarm.

Join the beta on TestFlight

Free on iPhone and iPad. Coming soon to the App Store.

Steer a pirate ship through cursed waters while your weapons fire automatically. Collect doubloons, choose a new blessing each time you level up, and outlast the swarm.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Pick your ship

Five free ships wait at your home port, from the nimble Swift Sloop to the armoured Geobukseon, a Korean turtle ship. Each has its own hull, speed and starting weapons.

Build your loadout

Combine cannon volleys, orbiting cannonballs, naval mines, lashing tentacles and lightning, and evolve them into stronger forms. Pick upgrades and passives to shape every run.

Face the cursed seas

Battle raiders, corsairs, sea creatures, ghost-ship bosses and the Kraken across animated waters and islands. Keep moving and see how long you can hold out.

Hunt and gamble

Race to guarded treasure before it sinks, or strike a bargain at a cursed island shrine for a bonus blessing. Both are optional.

Escape the Ferryman

Reach level 50, then outrun the Ferryman's flagship to the lighthouse harbour. Escape once to unlock Endless, where the voyage never stops.

Sail your way

Play with touch or a compatible controller, tune music, sound and haptics, and track every run in the Captain's Ledger, saved on your device.
