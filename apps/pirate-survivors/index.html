Pirate Survivors: Cursed Seas

Sail into cursed waters. Build your arsenal. Survive the swarm.

Coming soon to the App Store

Steer a pirate ship through cursed waters while your weapons fire automatically. Collect doubloons, choose a new blessing each time you level up, and outlast the swarm.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Build your loadout

Combine cannon volleys, orbiting cannonballs, naval mines, lashing tentacles and lightning. Pick upgrades and passives to shape every run.

Face the cursed seas

Battle waves of sea creatures and ghost-ship bosses across animated waters and islands. Keep moving and see how long you can hold out.

Sail your way

Play with touch controls or a compatible game controller, and tune music, sound effects and haptics to your liking.

Beat your own record

Every run is logged in the Captain's Ledger with your survival time, level and kills, saved on your device.
