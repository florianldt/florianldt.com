Pirate Survivors: Cursed Seas

Sail into cursed waters. Bring the flames home. Survive the swarm.

Join the beta on TestFlight

Free on iPhone and iPad. Coming soon to the App Store.

Steer a pirate ship through cursed waters while your weapons fire automatically. Gather soul-lights, choose a new blessing each time you level up, and outlast the swarm.

Play offline on iPhone and iPad. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Bring the flames home

The lighthouse went dark, and five drowned captains stole its flames. Cast off in the Swift Sloop, sink them one by one, and hear what each has to say before the fight.

Build your arsenal

Combine cannon volleys, grapeshot, orbiting cursed cannonballs, naval mines and chain lightning, and evolve them into the Explosive Cannon and the Storm Compass. Throw grappling hooks to board passing ships.

Face the drowned captains

A boss every ten levels, in a new order each voyage, among raiders, gunships, krakens and pufferfish. Ride out the swells and keep moving.

Hunt and gamble

Follow treasure maps to gold buried on distant islands, or strike a bargain at a cursed island shrine for a bonus blessing. Both are optional.

Escape the Ferryman

Reach level 60 and the Ferryman comes to collect. Slip through his soul-fire rings, break the three chains on his lantern and race to the blue harbor before time runs out.

Sail your way

Play with touch or a compatible controller, set music, sea, sound effects and menu sounds separately, and keep your best voyages in the Ledger, saved on your device.
